A place for boys to be made whole

This kids ranch will allow boys who have been separated from their parents for whatever reason to have a place to come and be restored. To be made whole so that they can have the good life that God designed for them.

A way to make restitution

It also allows those who have taken from others to make restitution by giving into this Jubilee Me project.

If you have harmed anyone, you should make restitution to them; this is part of making them whole.

But if you don't even know their name or where they live, how can you do that?

This project allows you to make restitution to a proxy young person who could never pay you back — and in that way, gives you an outlet to make restitution in a meaningful way.

Making restitution was designed by God to bring wholeness to everyone involved.

Be part of the restoration

Your gift helps purchase the land and build the place where restoration and restitution meet.

Help build a place where children are made whole. Your gift plants something eternal.
